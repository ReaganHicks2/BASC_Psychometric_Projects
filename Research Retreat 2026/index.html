Beyond the Manual: Examining the Structure of the BASC-3 in Neurodevelopmental and Standardization Samples​

Reagan M. Hicks, Eric A. Youngstrom, PhD, Eric M. Butter, PhD, Kevin G. Stephenson, PhD

Study Overview

The BASC-3 PRS-C factor structure showed limited replication across samples. A three-factor solution consistently outperformed the current scoring framework, raising concerns regarding the validity of existing composite scores and suggesting caution when interpreting BASC-3 composites in clinical decision-making, particularly for children referred for neurodevelopmental evaluation.
